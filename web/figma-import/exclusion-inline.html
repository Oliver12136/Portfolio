My Path

From an Exclusion Triggered by a “·”
To Designing from/for/with Margins

A middle dot should not decide whether someone is recognized.

Systems built without affected voices often reproduce exclusion.

As a Uyghur, I regularly encounter systems that reject my name because it includes a middle dot (·).

If I keep it, real-name verification fails. If I remove it, my name no longer matches my official documents.

What looks like a formatting detail is actually a design decision about who gets recognized and who gets filtered out.
